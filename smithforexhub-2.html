Forex learning academy

Beginner-friendly learning topics.

01 — What is forex?

Forex is the global market where currencies are exchanged. Prices are quoted in pairs, such as EUR/USD.

02 — Currency pairs

The first currency is the base currency; the second is the quote currency. A quote shows how much of the quote currency is needed for one unit of the base currency.

03 — Pips and spreads

A pip is a standard unit used to describe many exchange-rate movements. The spread is the difference between a quoted buy price and sell price.

04 — Risk management

Leverage can magnify both gains and losses. Never risk money you cannot afford to lose; a demo environment is best for learning.

05 — Trading psychology

Patience, planning and keeping records are important. Avoid impulsive decisions and promises of guaranteed profits.
