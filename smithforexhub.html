Market structure glossary

Higher high (HH): a swing high above a previous swing high.

Higher low (HL): a swing low above a previous swing low.

Lower high (LH): a swing high below a previous swing high.

Lower low (LL): a swing low below a previous swing low.

These terms describe historical price structure; they do not predict future movement.
